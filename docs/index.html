Outside the Lines · Hurricane Harvey Flood Explorer

Swipe between aerial photos of the same Houston-area spots before and during Harvey (Aug 2017). A vision AI model running locally on a Dell GB10 explains what changed — and flags flooding in places FEMA maps rated as lower risk.

AI assessments are generated by Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct on a Dell Pro Max GB10 and can be wrong — treat them as a starting point for human review, not an official damage survey. Before images are USDA NAIP (2016); response images are NOAA (Aug 30, 2017), so season and camera differ.

Data: UTSA flood images · NOAA GHCN-Daily rainfall · FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer · U.S. Census ACS · OpenStreetMap.
